# Governance and limitations

## Evidence boundary

- Balance-sheet/facility observations are deterministic synthetic data.
- Runoff, drawdown, rollover, haircut, ASF/RSF, recovery-capacity and EWI assumptions are illustrative.
- LCR/NSFR outputs are simplified analytical analogues, not regulatory reporting.
- No institution-specific liquidity adequacy, ILAAP compliance or recovery capacity is claimed.

## Production controls required

A production implementation would require legal-entity/perimeter mapping, product classification to the applicable rulebook, contractual and behavioral cash-flow reconciliation, encumbrance/collateral eligibility, intraday liquidity, operational deposits, contingent funding, currency mismatch, concentration, market depth, management-action feasibility, model validation, data lineage, auditability, limit governance and contingency-funding escalation.
